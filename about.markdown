# About Me

I am a student at Cal Poly with interests in GIS, environmental research, surveying, and spatial analysis.

My experience includes applied coursework, stakeholder-focused projects, fieldwork, and research related to environmental systems and geospatial technologies.

I am especially interested in work that combines spatial thinking, environmental analysis, and real-world problem solving.

## Skills and Interests

- Geographic Information Systems (GIS)
- Spatial analysis and mapping
- Environmental research
- Surveying and field data collection
- Technical communication
- Applied stakeholder projects

## Current Focus

I am continuing to develop experience in geospatial technologies, environmental analysis, and applied research through coursework, projects, and field-based work.
